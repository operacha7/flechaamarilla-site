Flecha Amarilla, Inc.

A Texas nonprofit corporation building free, practical tools that help people find the help they need.

Who we are

Flecha Amarilla, Inc. is a nonprofit corporation organized under the laws of the State of Texas and recognized as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. This website, flechaamarilla.org, is the official website of the corporation.

We are a small, volunteer-run organization. We do not charge for access to our programs, sell advertising, or sell data.

Our work

Community Resources Guide — Houston

A free directory that helps caseworkers, congregations, and neighbors find local assistance with rent, utilities, food, shelter, medical care, legal aid, immigration, and more — and share those resources directly with the people who need them.
